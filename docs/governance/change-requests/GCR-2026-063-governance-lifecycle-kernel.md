## Implementation and review boundaries

The adapter accepts only explicit synthetic GRS-002 reports and supplied fixture
Trust assertions. It preserves raw bytes and rejects missing criteria, mismatched
subjects and non-test contexts. It does not verify a real producer.

The store publishes one complete transaction atomically, under a local POSIX
lock, with no overwrite. Replay regenerates its classification, event and hash
chain. Duplicate accepted/quarantined deliveries are no-ops. New independent
failures increment one finding; incompatible or contradictory input is retained
for clarification without replacing accepted evidence. PASS does not close.

[ADR-CLG-002](../../operations/evidence/governance-lifecycle-kernel.md#git-acceptance-and-competing-prs-adr-clg-002)
resolves synthetic LD-06 using local serialization plus accepted-prefix and
merged-chain validation in the existing required Governance CI job. Two PRs
consuming the same revision fail after their histories are combined. This relies
on the existing strict/up-to-date checks; no settings or approvals are weakened.

The checked-in synthetic ledger and index are generated by the reproducible
demo script. They contain no findings against a live consumer and are not
connected to the existing viewer or official result indexes. Their namespace
refines the planned paths to keep synthetic and live state explicit.

Governance analysis preserves the reviewed plan and open authority decisions.
Evidence/intake review covers time/context, immutable evidence, quarantine and
latest selection. Release review finds no change to released packages, OPA,
consumer contracts or required migration. Repository stewardship requires full
validation, deterministic demo rebuild and exclusion of unrelated generated
metadata. Live profile/role changes, distributed storage, human decisions,
revocation, closure/reopening and waiver treatment remain later work.
